The letter $X$ of the split-signature arena $\Cl(3,3)$ has two readings: a length-valued displacement $X_{\rm cl}$, with converted times
on the temporal triple and lengths on the spatial one, and the field strength $X_{\rm EM}=(\bB,\bE)$, the pair labelling the six components
of the electromagnetic two-form. We type the two readings and show that no equivariant linear map relates them; we write the Lagrangians
of the worldline, of the interior shifted dynamics, of the real Dirac field and of the energies, and find that the rest energy
$E_0=\tfrac12|q|$ is a Lorentz-invariant and conformal but not a duality-invariant Lagrangian and not an admissible Hamiltonian; we construct
the four-momentum for a clock $n$ on the temporal triple, with the momenta conjugate to the three spatial lengths, and show that the mass
read by any clock is bounded by the six-dimensional mass; and we place the pair $(\bB,\bE)$ on the eight octants of the spinor module,
$\bE$ on the Fock weight one and $\bB$ on weight two, where Maxwell's equations become one first-order equation with the Gauss laws on the
poles while boosts and duality act only through odd operators. The temporal volume $T=e_1e_2e_3=nJ(n)$ is the element both readings share:
the Dirac mass form for every clock, the lift of the frame parity, and the duality operator on the octants; the Dirac field couples to the
field octants covariantly only through the Pauli term and its dual. Every finite-dimensional and symbolic claim is verified by script.
