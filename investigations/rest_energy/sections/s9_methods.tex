\section{How the statements were verified}
\label{s9:sec:methods}

Every finite-dimensional statement of \cref{d1:sec:main,d2:sec:breaks,d3:sec:main,d4:sec:bfield} was checked by computation, in two
independent passes. The scripts are in \texttt{investigations/rest\_energy/verify/} and run with \texttt{python3} from any directory, using
\texttt{numpy}, \texttt{sympy}, \texttt{scipy} and \texttt{mpmath}.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Derivation scripts} \texttt{d1\_unitary\_energy.py}, \texttt{d2\_symmetry\_breaking.py}, \texttt{d3\_closure\_dynamics.py} and
\texttt{d4\_bfield.py} were written together with the corresponding sections. They rebuild the realisation of \cite{ES17}, Appendix~A,
from its definition and print one \texttt{PASS} line per claim of their section, with the claim's label.
\item \emph{Adversarial scripts} \texttt{x1\_check.py} to \texttt{x4\_check.py} were written afterwards by separate authors whose task was to
refute the claims. They import nothing from the derivation scripts, rebuild every convention from the paper's sources, and wherever possible
use a different method: symbolic where the first pass was numerical, random tests where it used special cases, a different integrator for
the dynamics. Their findings, including one refuted claim and several reclassifications, were applied before the note was assembled.
\item \texttt{run\_all.py} runs all eight scripts and writes \texttt{RESULTS.md}; it exits with status zero only if every script does.
\end{enumerate}

Algebraic statements were checked exactly, in integer or rational arithmetic, since every blade of the realisation is a signed permutation
matrix. Statements about spectra and Jordan structure were checked with rational data; floating-point inputs were not used for
semisimplicity tests, where they produce false negatives. Dynamical statements were checked by numerical integration with relative
tolerances of $10^{-11}$ to $10^{-12}$, and elliptic and Mathieu quantities with \texttt{mpmath} and \texttt{scipy.special}. Statements of
\cite{ES17} that are cited without re-verification are marked as such in the text. The corrections made to \cite{ES17} itself in the course
of this work are listed, with their own checks, in \texttt{investigations/errata\_2026-10-17/}.
